# UnitedHealthcare — N130 Consult Plan Benefit Documents

N130 is a remark code instructing the provider to consult the plan benefit
documents or guidelines for restrictions on the service. It rides alongside a
CARC (often CO-96 non-covered or CO-197). The overturn turns the instruction
back on the payer: pull the actual plan benefit document, show there is no
restriction excluding the service, and appeal with the cited plan provision.

## Winning rebuttal

> We have consulted the plan benefit documents as remark N130 directs, and they
> contain no provision restricting or excluding this service for this member.
> The summary plan description lists the service as a covered benefit under the
> member's effective plan, with no limitation that would bar payment here.
> Because the plan documents support coverage rather than restrict it, we appeal
> the denial and request reprocessing with the cited plan-provision excerpt
> attached.

## Required evidence

- The member's summary plan description or evidence-of-coverage excerpt.
- The specific benefit section showing the service is covered, not excluded.
- The CARC that N130 accompanied, to address both codes in one appeal.

## IVR path

1. Dial the UnitedHealthcare provider line.
2. Press `1` for English.
3. Press `3` for "claims".
4. Say "benefit question" or press `5`.
5. Enter the tax ID or NPI followed by `#`.
6. Enter the member ID and date of birth as prompted.
7. Say "plan benefits" to reach the benefits desk.
8. Ask the representative to confirm the plan provision cited by remark N130 and
   request the benefit-document reference in writing.
