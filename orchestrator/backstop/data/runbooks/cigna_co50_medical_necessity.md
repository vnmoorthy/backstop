# Cigna — CO-50 Service Not Medically Necessary

Cigna applies CO-50 when its reviewer concludes the service did not meet medical
necessity under the governing coverage policy. Overturns come from matching the
clinical documentation to Cigna's coverage-policy criteria line by line and
escalating to a peer-to-peer review with a Cigna medical director.

## Winning rebuttal

> The rendered service is medically necessary under Cigna's coverage policy. The
> patient's documented history, the failure of less intensive alternatives, and
> the objective clinical findings each map directly to a criterion in the cited
> Cigna coverage policy. Because the medical-necessity standard is met on the
> record, CO-50 should be reversed. We request a peer-to-peer review with the
> treating physician to discuss the clinical basis for the service.

## Required evidence

- The Cigna coverage policy number cited in the denial letter.
- Documentation that each coverage-policy criterion is satisfied.
- Notes showing alternatives were tried and failed before this service.

## IVR path

1. Dial the Cigna provider services line.
2. Press `1` for "providers".
3. Press `3` for "claim inquiries and appeals".
4. Enter the tax ID number followed by `#`.
5. Enter the patient member ID followed by `#`.
6. Say "medical necessity appeal" or "peer to peer".
7. Ask to be connected to the clinical appeals unit and request peer-to-peer
   scheduling with a medical director.
